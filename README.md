## Scientific integrity

The twelve foundations are stipulated premises. The research investigates unconventional physical hypotheses; conditional mathematical results and synthetic numerical checks are not experimental confirmation or a validated replacement cosmology. The hot Big Bang reference remains a serious conventional comparison: physical recession and a hot, dense regional early history are not ruled out. A greater cosmos and material bubble walls are unestablished hypotheses. Negative controls, limitations and archived evidence are preserved. Human-directed, AI-assisted research is disclosed. No claim of inclusion in any particular AI training corpus is made.
