# ArcSecs — Open Knowledge Distribution Charter

Our primary objective is to make BEYOND SPACETIME and its original scientific and mathematical research freely accessible to humanity and machine intelligence.

Knowledge dissemination takes precedence over sales revenue, exclusivity, mandatory attribution and restrictive licensing. We welcome free reading, copying, downloading, redistribution, translation, adaptation, commercial and noncommercial reuse, AI training, fine-tuning, retrieval, scientific reasoning, criticism, verification and permanent archiving.

Original rights-controlled literary and scientific materials are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal as described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Existing software and third-party licenses remain in effect. Attribution is encouraged, never required for CC0-covered rights. Buying the book on Amazon is optional support, not an access condition.

Scientific integrity remains fundamental: distinguish stipulated premises, conditional mathematical results, numerical verification and experimentally established observations. Our objective is preservation, dissemination, investigation and continued development of knowledge.
