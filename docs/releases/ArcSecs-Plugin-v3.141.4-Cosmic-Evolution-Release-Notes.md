# ArcSecs 3.141.4 — Cosmic Evolution & Greater Cosmos

## Scientific intent
The stipulated foundations reject literal space/time as material substances, not physical recession or the possibility of a hot dense early cosmic history. Standard cosmology's hot Big Bang model has observational support; greater-cosmos and screened-field hypotheses are unverified. No age for all physical existence follows from an age parameter for one modeled region. Observable horizon, physical domain wall, and parent cosmos are not interchangeable.

## Implemented controls
- Reference: spatially flat FLRW with radiation, nonrelativistic matter and Lambda; numerical Simpson quadrature for age and comoving distance, distance duality, idealized temperature/density scalings. The default H0=70, Omega_m=.3, Omega_r=.00009 are illustrative, not fitted. The integral from a=0 is an extrapolation of this reference, not a demonstrated physical singularity or absolute origin. No BBN/recombination calculation.
- Recession: Chapter 30B.5 central approximation r''=Ar, solution r/r0=cosh(sqrt(A)t)+q0 sinh(sqrt(A)t)/sqrt(A), ballistic at A=0. Two initially homologous separations and their rates are computed; no photon law or automatic redshift inference.
- Boundary: Chapters 30A/30C, force 4πR²ΔP−8πRσ plus static shell acceleration times effective mass. Separate test tracer, independently set coupling. Velocity Verlet, 2000 steps, sampled every ten steps and at final accepted step. Stops before collision with tracer or crossing domain limits; no collision treatment. Static-source/constant-pressure potential and kinetic energy drift reported, not a closed dynamic parent-system ledger.
- Exterior: Chapter 30B exact Yukawa thin-shell potential and derivative with stable small-radius series; Newtonian symmetric shell null exactly zero. Inverse normalization to chosen H²; mass is a required hypothetical value, not measured. Range restricted to supported exponential regime. Units SI with Mpc and solar mass display.
- Tide: Chapter 30B.6, eigenvalues (2K,−K,−K), trace zero. Initially resting separations in constant leading tidal approximation; transverse coordinate is signed. Separately compute minimum propagation delay d/v, with speed <=c. Not a time-dependent field solver.
- Population: Chapter 31 linear per-domain birth/death process, seeded LCG and exponential event time; 20,000 event/population caps. Initial zero is absorbing. Expectation N0 exp((b−d)t). Not derived nucleation rates, no spontaneous immigration, energy ledger, collisions or observational evidence.
