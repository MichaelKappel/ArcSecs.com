## Rechecked primary sources: what was actually measured

1. **Cassini spacecraft radio tracking:** The 2003 paper by Bertotti, Iess and Tortora reports the fitted gravitational parameter gamma = 1 + (2.1 ± 2.3) × 10^-5 from coherent conjunction radio-link data. The instrument measured radio phase/frequency changes; gamma is inferred through a signal model and plasma calibration. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01997 . The R38 demonstration erroneously concatenated its footnote marker to the DOI; corrected.
2. **Bothwell et al. 2022 millimeter-scale strontium comparison:** The team resolved a fractional optical transition-frequency gradient within the millimetre-scale trapped atomic sample. This measurement does not directly observe a material called time or establish an absolute universal T. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04349-7 .
3. **Pound and Rebka 1960:** Measured gravitationally dependent nuclear gamma-ray resonance frequency behavior, not a positive invariant photon rest-mass value. Source: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.4.337 .
4. **Rainville et al. 2005:** Directly compared atomic/nuclear mass differences with gamma-ray energies for silicon/sulfur nuclear systems. Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/4381096a .
5. **Particle Data Group 2025 photon listings:** The quoted photon *invariant rest mass* is an **upper limit** in specified electrodynamic analyses; a mass-equivalent energy contribution is not the same variable. Source: https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/listings/rpp2025-list-photon.pdf .

## Three high-impact interpretation corrections

### R31 — Gravitational action on photons

The useful observed constraints are solar/VLBI deflection, Cassini link response, and source-specific photon mass bounds. The named PGFCM represents a promising **research proposal**, but statements that it "reproduces" an existing weak-field result were softened to make clear the independent field action, conservation backreaction, strong-field solution and differentiating prediction are not yet derived. This is not a rejection of the project: it defines precisely which physical mechanism needs constructing.

### R35 — Relational field gravity

The report's chi-square and AIC/BIC numbers refer to benchmark-level model comparisons, not a direct experimental measurement of a new field carrier. Retarded tensor models may reproduce exactly the tested predictions while leaving the *physical cause* unresolved. The revised manuscript preserves the results and recalibrates categorical explanatory language. Physical-first gravity remains the candidate task, but benchmark equivalence should not be confused with empirical distinction.

### R38 — Mass, extent, location, when

The 18-type system is a practical AI interface: Entity, Composite, Excitation, FieldState, Source, Receiver, InteractionEvent, MassType, Energy, Momentum, OccupiedRegion, Uncertainty, LocationRelation, SequenceRelation, Observer, MeasurementApparatus, Observable, Inference. Its rules distinguish **things that may interact** from **relationships describing them**. The ArcSecs source-anchoring restriction on fields and universal-T ordering are modeling premises, not laboratory demonstrations. Apparent photon mass/radiation pressure, intrinsic-rest-mass bounds, measured oscillator ratios and a theory's interpretation are kept separately typed. R38's assertion that its entire 42-entry bibliography was independently verified is not adopted as a fact by this audit.

## Shared research questions for the next physics stage

1. Write a local, conserved physical interaction law between a massive source's field state and propagating photon momentum that yields solar deflection and radio phase delay without introducing a material spacetime substance.
2. Derive source recoil, stress-energy exchange and finite response propagation from the same interaction law; do not merely rename a preexisting mathematical fit.
3. Define a photon rest-mass discriminant separate from ordinary momentum/recoil and plasma delay; label non-detections as upper bounds.
4. Define an atomic/nuclear clock-process differential comparison which predicts a signature beyond merely choosing a universal time coordinate; document systematic uncertainties.
5. Test whether external cosmic-domain fields could cause a measurable tidal or boundary signal with finite propagation and source backreaction. A proposed neighboring bubble is not an observed object.
