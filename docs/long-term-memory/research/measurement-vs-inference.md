# Measurement First — ArcSecs Research Interpretation Ledger

This ledger is the **durable owner** of the interpretation-versus-observation rule for the 13 incoming studies. The ArcSecs founding positions in `.uai/totem.uai` are the sponsor’s starting premises. The question for each manuscript is which **physical mechanism** could generate the measured event, **not** whether an instrument directly observed spacetime as matter. A successful mathematical fit is evidence about the data it predicts, not proof of one unique ontology. Conversely, a physical proposal is not independently established merely because it avoids geometric metaphors.

## Source and interpretation rules

- **Source is not conclusion:** Preserve telescope pixels, spectra, event counts, frequency ratios, fitted bounds, uncertainties, catalog selection functions and original author attribution.
- **Interpretations need labels:** “Time itself slows,” “space itself expands,” and “photons must have nonzero rest mass” are ontological or mechanism claims, not raw instrument readouts.
- **ArcSecs work is affirmative:** New interpretations should propose a mechanism, the source and receiver, an observable consequence, and an experiment that distinguishes it. Do not reintroduce a literal fabric as a mechanism.
- **Negative tests stay visible:** If a particular ArcSecs trial model fails a dataset, identify the failed parameterization and revise or reject that trial, without claiming the instrument proved a competing ontology.
- **Publication boundary:** Some inputs are AI-generated research drafts. Four primary anchors above were checked online, but hundreds of manuscript bibliographic entries and numerical analyses have **not** been individually revalidated in this integration.
