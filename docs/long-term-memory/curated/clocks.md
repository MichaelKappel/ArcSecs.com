**Status:** research premise and observable.  **Input:** T-M L5–19; C01-H L726–728; D-H L1212–1224.

T is the proposed constant ordering coordinate (When); x labels Where. A local physical clock records N_j=integral nu_j(x,v,T)dT; for radians divide phase by 2 pi. Reproduce measured rate ratios for optical, microwave, nuclear and unstable-particle clocks. Standard relativistic predictions are comparison controls, not an ontological command to discard the charter.

## T-03 — Moving sources plus weak transport aging
**Status:** analytic and test gate.  **Input:** T-M L35–53; P-H L87–94; P-H L498–514; P-M L51–67.

Prioritize a hybrid model in which physical recession produces arrival stretch while a physically justified bath contributes a separately computed propagation-energy shift. Stationary identical packets at stationary endpoints have dT_a/dT_e=1, regardless of energy loss. If stable clock standards and an assumed kinematic sector give A=1+z_kin, with (1+z)=(1+z_kin)(1+z_diss), then A=(1+z)/(1+z_diss). The effective exponent is ln A/ln(1+z); use the DES time-stretch likelihood, not a universal fixed 0.5–1% fraction copied from an explorer. The latter requires a specific redshift/fraction model, covariance and confidence level. 30R derives moving-endpoint timing; 30S tests microscopic recoil. For one-dimensional forward propagation in a common coordinate frame, let signed source and receiver velocities be w_e and w_a (positive toward the receiver). On a smooth ray family satisfying x_r(T_a)-x_e(T_e)=integral from T_e to T_a of v(T;T_e)dT, implicit differentiation gives A=[v(T_e;T_e)-w_e-integral of partial_(T_e) v(T;T_e)dT]/[v(T_a;T_e)-w_a], provided the denominator is nonzero and the same arrival branch is followed. For an age-only speed law v(T-T_e), A=(v_arr-w_e)/(v_arr-w_a); for a common absolute-time law v(T), A=(v_emit-w_e)/(v_arr-w_a). Physical clock interval ratio multiplies A by nu_receiver/nu_emitter; spectral frequency instead follows local phase rate omega-k dot w and clock calibration. These are distinct observables. Do not multiply energy loss by an independent arrival factor without deriving it. The original pulse formula and assertion of verified DES agreement are not promoted.
