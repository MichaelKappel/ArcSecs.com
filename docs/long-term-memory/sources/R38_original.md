## **Uncertainty, Controls, Limitations, and Candid Verdict**

Evaluating all evidence together reveals four substantial empirical and methodological limitations. First, in precision optical lattice metrology (Bothwell et al. 2022), raw statistical uncertainty reached 7.6 times ten to the minus 21, yet the systematic uncertainty on the linear gradient—from density shifts, lattice light shifts, blackbody radiation, and imaging calibration—was 2.3 times ten to the minus 20 per millimeter against a corrected signal of negative 9.8 times ten to the minus 20 per millimeter7. Any differential species comparison (Test 1\) demands strict magic-wavelength and density-matching controls so systematic drifts do not masquerade as species-dependent physical couplings.  
Second, severe astrophysical model degeneracies complicate interpretation of published limits. Torsion-balance photon mass bounds (1.2 × 10⁻⁵¹ grams in Luo et al. 200315) depend on assumed cosmic magnetic vector potentials37, FRB dispersion bounds (3.9 × 10⁻⁵⁰ kilograms in Bonetti et al. 201711) depend on intergalactic plasma separation and cosmic expansion models43, GW170817/GRB 170817A speed bounds depend on astrophysical jet-emission delay modeling up to 10 seconds10, and Tolman surface-brightness interpretations diverge between Lerner et al. Third, a sharp boundary separates representational novelty from empirical novelty. As a substrate-free modeling architecture, the ArcSecs "What, Where, and When" ontology is internally consistent. Yet unless paired with verified species-dependent clock shifts, a verified physical energy sink for secular light slowdown, or a confirmed nonzero invariant photon rest mass, it produces identical local weak-field predictions to standard coordinate benchmarks. ## **Companion Specification: REFEREE\_CHALLENGES.md**

**Challenge 1: Does replacing "spacetime" with "What, Where, and When" change a single empirical prediction, or is it merely a semantic relabeling of general relativity?**  
*Response:* By itself, renaming spatial coordinates as relational "Where" and progression parameter **T** as "When" is empirically equivalent to standard coordinate descriptions and changes zero predictions. This report explicitly disallows claiming empirical confirmation from ontology alone. Empirical identifiability enters exclusively through the testable physical interaction hypotheses paired with the ontology in Work Package G—specifically testing whether co-located clocks of distinct atomic species couple identically to gravitational potentials (EXP-01), whether photons display Proca rest-mass dispersion or secular energy loss in blind archival splits (EXP-02), and whether controlled local vector potentials exert Proca torques (EXP-03).  
**Challenge 2: Premise 1 claims photons have mass, yet the Particle Data Group (2024/2025) sets an upper bound below 1 × 10⁻¹⁸ eV and FRBs push below 10⁻⁵⁰ kg. Isn't Premise 1 already disproven?**  
*Response:* Precision classification separates confirmed measurements from open bounds. Experiments confirm that photons transfer momentum in dielectric media (Campbell et al. 200513) and exhibit apparent gravitational frequency shifts (Pound and Rebka 196014), establishing nonzero **RadiativeFieldMass**. What remains undetected and tightly bounded is nonzero **InvariantRestMass**. Rather than pretending an unperformed detection succeeded, our typed schema enforces this exact distinction, tagging invariant rest mass as BoundedOpen while designing model-independent laboratory tests (EXP-03) that remove astrophysical vector-potential assumptions.  
**Challenge 3: If an electron shows no substructure down to 10⁻¹⁹–10⁻²⁰ meters at LEP2 (Bourilkov 2001), how can Premise 6 claim that everything physically existing occupies area at every moment?**  
*Response:* High-energy scattering cross sections probe momentum transfer at short distances, not the geometric boundary of an isolated classical sphere. Bound or trapped electrons occupy nonzero quantum spatial localization domains (such as the Bohr radius scale of 5.29 × 10⁻¹¹ meters5) and maintain surrounding electromagnetic **FieldStates** carrying energy across finite regions. Premise 6 fails only if "occupying area" is conflated with a rigid solid sphere; it holds operationally when **OccupiedRegion** is defined via quantum spatial support and measurable field boundaries.  
**Challenge 4: If time is truly constant (Premise 2), how do you explain Bothwell et al. (2022) measuring a frequency gradient across a single 1-millimeter atomic cloud?**  
*Response:* Bothwell et al. (2022) measured a fractional difference in atomic fluorescence transition frequencies (-9.8(2.3) × 10⁻²⁰ per mm)7, not a direct measurement of a physical "time substance" stretching. Within constant universal **T**, all atoms in the millimeter lattice evolve synchronously along the same progression index, while the local gravitational **FieldState** perturbs the internal transition energetics of the lower atoms relative to the upper atoms. Measuring whether that physical oscillator shift is identical across disparate atomic and nuclear transitions (EXP-01) directly tests this physical mechanism against metric universality.  
**Challenge 5: If Premise 3 ("Light slows down over time") were true, wouldn't LHAASO observations of multi-TeV photons from GRB 221009A and the 1.74-second GW170817/GRB 170817A coincidence immediately rule it out?**  
*Response:* Those observations impose severe constraints on Premise 3\. LHAASO (Cao et al. 2024\) limits energy-dependent photon speed dispersion across more than 5,000 very-high-energy photons12, and GW170817/GRB 170817A constrains any speed gap between gravity and gamma rays over more than 26 megaparsecs to parts in ten to the minus fifteen10. Accordingly, Work Package E explicitly flags the **missing physical mechanism** for Premise 3: any viable secular slowdown mechanism must transfer energy to an explicit physical sink without inducing spectral broadening or violating laboratory atomic constants—an unestablished requirement targeted for falsification in EXP-02.  
**Challenge 6: Without a vacuum zero-point substance, how does the ArcSecs ontology account for Casimir forces measured between neutral plates?**  
*Response:* Precision Casimir force measurements (Bressi et al. 200249) occur strictly between material conducting boundaries, and theoretical analyses examine both vacuum-energy and boundary-scattering descriptions of the interaction (Jaffe 200551; scattering formulations54). Within the ArcSecs physical-first hypothesis, empty coordinates contribute nothing physically; the measured attraction is attributed to a source-anchored electromagnetic **FieldState** spanning the physical gap between charges and currents in the bounding plates.  
**Challenge 7: Doesn't Deser (1970) prove that a consistent spin-2 gravitational field on flat space inevitably reduces to curved Riemannian spacetime, making the flat background unobservable?**  
*Response:* Deser (1970) demonstrated that a *massless, universally coupled* spin-2 field generates equations algebraically isomorphic to metric gravity, rendering the underlying flat background metric unobservable *within that universal-coupling limit*45. That result reinforces our exact identifiability warning: if a physical FieldState couples universally with identical strength to all matter and radiation, its kinematics reproduce standard metric predictions. Only if physical couplings exhibit species dependence, finite-range Yukawa cutoffs, or preferred-frame effects can experiments distinguish a physical-field ontology from geometric metric theories.  
**Challenge 8: How do you address Premises 8 and 9 ("Light is not the fastest thing; things move apart faster than causality") without invoking expanding space?**  
*Response:* We do not rescue Premises 8 and 9 via expanding space (forbidden by the charter). Instead, we record candidly that GW170817/GRB 170817A constrains gravitational versus electromagnetic propagation speeds to parts in ten to the minus fifteen10, that Bell correlation experiments across 1.3 kilometers (Hensen et al. 201564) exhibit statistical correlation without superluminal energy transfer, and that **missing physical mechanisms** remain for both faster-than-light causal signal transport and super-causal separation of physical bodies.
