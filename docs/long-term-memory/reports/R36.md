## **Abstract**

This investigation evaluates whether a physical source-to-receiver photon energy-loss or velocity-damping mechanism operating within constant universal progression and non-material Euclidean spatial coordinates can replace or supplement expanding-space redshift across five empirical benchmarks: the redshift–distance relation, Type Ia supernova light-curve durations, Tolman surface brightness, cosmic microwave background (CMB) spectral and angular-power structure, and high-redshift image sharpness, alongside baryon acoustic oscillations and multimessenger arrival timing. Analyzing two specified microphysical channels—forward-coherent dynamical scalar-field drag and collective intergalactic plasma-wake excitation—plus a physical-recession hybrid, we find that **pure constant-speed photon energy loss is quantitatively excluded** as a primary redshift mechanism. Against the Dark Energy Survey five-year measurement of 1,504 Type Ia supernovae, where observed light-curve durations widen as one plus redshift raised to 1.003 ± 0.005 (statistical) ± 0.010 (systematic), a non-stretching prediction of zero incurs a chi-squared penalty of 4,471.2 (66.87 sigma) under linear error summation and 8,048.1 (89.71 sigma) under quadrature1. Any constant-speed photon-loss component is bounded to at most 1.94 to 2.70 percent of total logarithmic redshift at redshift one (95 percent two-sigma confidence)2. Two-body incoherent scattering predicts multi-degree angular blurring3 excluded by sharp sub-arcsecond galaxy and quasar imaging4, while energy-only redshifting of a thermal emitter violates the 50 parts-per-million COBE/FIRAS blackbody limit beyond redshift 0.00001673. The most promising ArcSecs candidate is **forward-coherent secular velocity-damped scalar transport**, which widens arrival intervals linearly with one plus redshift without stretching time itself, yet lacks a verified mechanism that shifts optical grating wavelengths and radio heterodyne frequencies equally, matches gravitational-wave speeds to one part in a quadrillion7, and generates CMB acoustic peaks8. Its single cheapest decisive test is an archival cross-band comparison of optical grating versus radio heterodyne absorber redshifts above redshift one.

## **2\. Literature Review and Observational Requirements Register**

### **2.1 Historical and Modern Photon-Loss Proposals**

Physical photon-loss accounts of cosmological redshift originated with Zwicky (1929), who proposed gravitational drag on light traversing intergalactic matter4. Subsequent critiques established that classical two-body scattering transfers transverse momentum to photons, blurring point-like distant sources, whereas observations demonstrate sharp high-redshift quasar and galaxy profiles (Steinbring 20074). Later alternative proposals revisited static or hybrid mechanisms, including plasma-redshift electron interactions16, double-Compton scattering conjectures18, exponential wavelength-distance relations19, and the Covarying Coupling Constants plus Tired Light (CCC+TL) hybrid proposed by Gupta (2023) to address compact high-redshift galaxies observed by the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)22.  
Independent empirical checks have challenged recent hybrid models. A 2026 peer-reviewed evaluation in *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* showed that Gupta’s best-fit supernova parameters diverge significantly from observational cosmic-chronometer expansion-rate measurements, concluding that compact high-redshift galaxies seen with JWST are more consistent with early intrinsic galaxy evolution23. Separately, Vavryčuk (2025) argued that standard expanding-ray kinematics keep successive photon intervals unchanged unless a rate-varying propagation effect operates, advancing a conformal varying-speed-of-light metric26. While illustrating active inquiry into light transport, none of those prior formulations has simultaneously satisfied supernova duration scaling, Tolman surface brightness, and CMB blackbody and acoustic-peak measurements with a single specified physical sink.

## **4\. Mechanism Discovery and Physical Transport Analysis**

### **4.1 Minimal Phenomenological Attenuation and Exclusion of Particle Scattering**

Starting from the observed redshift ratio and a minimal phenomenological attenuation law in which fractional photon energy loss per unit Euclidean path length equals a constant negative coefficient, integration yields exponential energy decay with path length and a logarithmic distance–redshift relation. Matching the low-redshift Hubble slope of 67.4 kilometers per second per megaparsec36 requires a characteristic attenuation length of 4,448 megaparsecs, or a fractional loss rate of 7.29 × 10^-27 per meter. By itself, however, this attenuation coefficient is **strictly a curve fit** that specifies neither an energy recipient nor momentum conservation.  
Evaluating standard two-body incoherent scattering (including conventional Compton scattering on free electrons, protons, or hypothetical light particles, which is standard electrodynamics rather than new physics) demonstrates an immediate kinematic impossibility. In elastic two-body collisions where optical photons near two electronvolts lose a small fraction of energy per scatter, the tiny single-scatter deflection angle requires proportionally more collisions to accumulate a cosmological redshift of one. When summed in quadrature across all collisions along the line of sight, the collision count cancels out identically: the root-mean-square total angular blur depends only on the square root of the target rest mass divided by the photon energy3. For optical light at redshift one, this yields 595.1 radians (122.8 million arcseconds) on electrons, 25,500 radians (5.26 billion arcseconds) on protons, 0.833 radians (171,700 arcseconds) on a one-electronvolt particle, and 0.0263 radians (5,430 arcseconds, or 1.5 degrees) even on a one-millielectronvolt particle3. Because distant quasars and galaxies remain crisp without cosmological blurring down to sub-arcsecond imaging scales (Steinbring 20074; JWST NIRCam5), **every incoherent two-body recoil mechanism is excluded by four to ten orders of magnitude**.

### **4.2 Mechanism 1: Forward-Coherent Scalar-Field Drag and Secular Velocity Damping**

To preserve sub-arcsecond image sharpness while respecting the ArcSecs requirement that all physical entities occupy area and carry mass-energy, the first specified microphysical channel couples a massive electromagnetic vector field (bounded in invariant rest mass below 2.2 × 10^-15 electronvolts divided by speed of light squared12) to a smooth, pervasive cosmic scalar field via a derivative interaction in the Lagrangian (derived in analysis/mechanisms\_and\_checks.py). Because the cosmic scalar gradient is spatially homogeneous over galactic scales, transverse momentum transfer vanishes identically. In the resulting radiative-transfer kinetic equation, photon occupation undergoes pure frequency drift with zero angular diffusion and zero spectral-line broadening beyond intrinsic emitter profiles, transferring lost photon energy directly into the kinetic energy density of the scalar field without emitting secondary X-rays.  
Separating this scalar interaction into two kinematic regimes reveals the decisive physics of ArcSecs Premise 3 (“Light slows down over time”):  
**Mechanism 1A (Constant-Speed Forward Scalar Drag):** When the scalar field extracts photon energy at constant propagation speed, consecutive photons emitted across a supernova light curve traverse identical Euclidean distances in equal durations, forcing a predicted duration-scaling exponent of zero. Tested against the DES measurement of 1.003 ± 0.005 (statistical) ± 0.010 (systematic)2, constant-speed drag suffers a chi-squared penalty of 40,240.4 (200.60 sigma) statistically, 8,048.1 (89.71 sigma) under quadrature, and 4,471.2 (66.87 sigma) under linear error summation3. In addition, pure energy-loss luminosity distance underpredicts supernova distance moduli by 0.611 magnitudes at redshift 0.5, 0.966 magnitudes at redshift 1.0, and 1.374 magnitudes at redshift 2.0 relative to benchmark expansion curves; even adding an ad hoc photon-number depletion factor leaves a systematic residual of minus 0.213 magnitudes at redshift 1.03. Likewise, bolometric surface brightness falls as only one power of one plus redshift, excluded at more than 10 sigma by Lubin and Sandage (2001)37.  
**Mechanism 1B (Secular Velocity-Damped Scalar Transport — Most Promising Channel):** If instead the scalar condensate lowers the effective electromagnetic propagation speed monotonically over universal progression (while Premise 8 permits faster causal speeds above today’s electromagnetic speed), differentiating the fixed-distance Euclidean transit integral with respect to emission instant yields a simple, exact kinematic identity: **the ratio of observed arrival duration to emitted duration equals the ratio of emission light speed to detection light speed**. Whenever propagation speed slows by a factor of one plus redshift between emission and arrival, supernova light-curve widths and quasar variability timescales widen linearly with one plus redshift without stretching time or space.  
However, confronting Mechanism 1B with the remaining benchmarks exposes **three unresolved microphysical gaps**:

> 1. **Grating Wavelength Versus Heterodyne Frequency Divergence:** In a spatially homogeneous, time-varying refractive scalar medium, spatial translation symmetry conserves photon wavevector, leaving diffraction-grating wavelengths fixed at their emitted values while temporal variation lowers detected frequencies. Optical grating spectrographs and radio heterodyne receivers would therefore measure discrepant redshifts unless an additional spatial-dispersion coupling operates—yet simultaneously stretching wavelength and slowing propagation speed reduces frequency by the square of one plus redshift, overpredicting supernova light-curve widths by a factor of two.  
> 2. **GW170817 Speed-Coincidence Constraint:** Because gravitational waves from GW170817 and gamma rays from GRB 170817A arrived within 1.74 seconds across extragalactic distance, bounding their fractional speed difference between minus 3 × 10^-15 and plus 7 × 10^-167, any secular slowing of light over universal progression must either slow gravitational waves identically to 15 decimal places or remain below 10^-15 across that baseline.  
> 3. **CMB Photon-Number Dilution and Acoustic Peaks:** Even if the CMB began as a hotter blackbody emitter whose photon energies dropped uniformly by one plus redshift without reducing photon number density, the received specific intensity would retain the emitter’s cubed higher photon density, producing a massive greybody excess. Against the 50 parts-per-million COBE/FIRAS residual ceiling6, pure energy-only redshifting of a blackbody emitter is restricted to redshifts below 0.00001673, and scalar drag provides no acoustic mechanism for the Planck 2018 multi-peak angular spectra8.

### **4.3 Mechanism 2: Collective Plasma-Wake and Plasmon-Excitation Drag**

A second microphysical channel replaces scalar fields with known ionized intergalactic plasma, coupling the electromagnetic field minimally to collective longitudinal electron oscillations (plasmons)16. Lost photon energy heats intergalactic electrons, generating thermodynamic entropy inversely proportional to the intergalactic plasma temperature. Quantitative transport analysis excludes collective plasma drag on four independent fronts: (1) plasma dispersion and wake cross-sections vary inversely with frequency squared10, violating observed equality between radio, optical, and gamma-ray redshifts; (2) depositing cosmic radiative energy loss into intergalactic electrons drives Comptonization distortions orders of magnitude beyond the COBE/FIRAS 15 parts-per-million limit39 and violates localized FRB electron-column dispersion bounds10; (3) finite-wavevector plasmon recoil induces Debye-scale angular blur and line broadening; and (4) constant-speed plasma drag predicts zero supernova duration stretching, excluded at 66.87 to 200.60 sigma3.

### **4.4 Mechanism 3: Physical Euclidean Recession Plus Forward Scalar Drag (Hybrid)**

Consistent with ArcSecs Premise 9—that physical bodies move apart through Euclidean spatial coordinates faster than causal signal speeds without stretching space or time—Mechanism 3 combines physical radial recession with weak forward-coherent scalar drag. Radial separation lengthens sequential photon travel paths, naturally producing linear arrival-interval widening for the kinematic recession component while scalar drag adds a non-stretching multiplicative redshift factor. Under this hybrid, the effective duration-scaling exponent equals one minus the non-stretching photon-loss fraction of logarithmic redshift. Applying a conservative two-sigma lower bound to White et al.’s (2024) measurement (1.003 ± 0.01118 quadrature or ± 0.015 linear) constrains any non-stretching tired-light contribution to **at most 1.94 percent (quadrature) or 2.70 percent (linear)** of total logarithmic redshift out to redshift one2. While Euclidean recession plus trace scalar drag accommodates low-redshift Hubble curves, it still requires an independent hot ionized-plasma acoustic phase to explain Planck 2018 CMB multipole oscillations8 and DESI DR2 baryon acoustic distance ratios44.

## **Companion File: ADVOCACY\_SUMMARY.md (Plain-English ArcSecs Evidence Summary)**

### **What We Tested and Why It Matters**

Under the ArcSecs framework, space and time are not physical fabrics that can stretch; instead, “Where” denotes geometric position and “When” denotes a constant universal progression. Because light carries mass-energy and slows down over cosmic progression under ArcSecs premises, Independent Mission 04 tested whether a physical interaction between traveling photons and fields or matter can explain why distant galaxies appear redshifted without invoking expanding space.

### **What the Observations Show**

Two precision measurements place severe empirical constraints on traditional non-expansion photon-loss models. First, across 1,504 Type Ia supernovae measured by the Dark Energy Survey out to redshift \~1.2, distant explosions rise and fade more slowly in direct proportion to one plus redshift (exponent 1.003 ± 0.0152). A constant-speed photon energy-loss model predicts zero widening of explosion durations and fails that dataset by more than 66 standard deviations; even in a hybrid model, non-stretching photon energy loss cannot exceed roughly 2 to 3 percent of total redshift3. Second, if photons lost energy by colliding with electrons, protons, or even ultra-light particles, distant galaxies and quasars would blur across thousands to billions of arcseconds, contradicting sharp astronomical imaging3. Likewise, reducing photon energies in a static volume without reducing photon numbers breaks the cosmic microwave background’s 50 parts-per-million blackbody spectrum beyond tiny local redshifts3.

### **The Most Promising ArcSecs Path—and Its Cheapest Test**

Constant-speed “tired light” is excluded. However, **ArcSecs Premise 3 (“Light slows down over time”)** identifies a sharp physical opening: if a pervasive physical scalar field monotonically slows light’s propagation speed over constant progression, trailing photons from a supernova take longer to complete the journey than leading photons, **widening observed light-curve durations by one plus redshift without stretching time itself**. To mature into a viable theory, this slowing-light mechanism must still explain how diffraction-grating wavelengths and radio frequencies shift equally, why gravitational waves and gamma rays from GW170817 arrived in lockstep to one part in a quadrillion7, and how the cosmic microwave background acquires its blackbody density and acoustic peaks6. We can test the core slowing-light prediction immediately at near-zero cost by comparing optical grating redshifts against radio heterodyne redshifts in archival spectra of distant gas clouds above redshift one.

## **Companion File: REFEREE\_CHALLENGES.md**

> 1. **Grating-Wavelength Versus Heterodyne-Frequency Paradox:** A spatially homogeneous, time-varying refractive scalar medium conserves photon wavevector, keeping optical grating wavelengths unshifted while lowering radio heterodyne frequencies. Conversely, stretching wavelength while slowing propagation speed reduces frequency by the square of one plus redshift, predicting a supernova duration exponent of two—excluded by DES. What physical mechanism in Euclidean coordinates shifts both grating wavelength and heterodyne frequency by exactly one plus redshift while producing a duration exponent of unity?  
> 2. **GW170817 / GRB 170817A Co-Propagation Bound:** GW170817 and GRB 170817A arrived within 1.74 seconds across extragalactic distance, fixing the fractional difference between gravitational and electromagnetic speeds between minus 3 × 10^-15 and plus 7 × 10^-167. How can electromagnetic waves slow substantially over cosmic progression to generate cosmological redshift without desynchronizing from gravitational waves?  
> 3. **Planckian Photon-Number Conservation ((1 \+ z)^3 Excess):** Under this report’s specified unchanged-number-density/intensity-normalization toy model, a hotter initial blackbody develops a greybody excess; the 50-parts-per-million threshold near redshift 0.00001673 is conditional on that radiative-transfer assumption and is not a universal limit on all coherent photon-transfer laws. What achromatic physical sink removes the exact excess fraction of CMB photons without distorting the Planck curve?  
> 4. **Origin of Planck 2018 Acoustic Peaks and DESI DR2 BAO Ruler:** Planck 2018 temperature and polarization spectra8 and DESI DR2 galaxy and Lyman-alpha clustering correlations42 independently recover a coherent physical sound-horizon scale across gigaparsec volumes. Without an early coupled hot-plasma epoch, what physical mechanism in static or Euclidean recession produces correlated harmonic acoustic oscillations?  
> 5. **Tolman Surface Brightness Dilution:** Even when secular velocity damping stretches arrival rates and lowers photon energies by one factor of one plus redshift each, static Euclidean geometry still spreads flux over area proportional to distance squared rather than distance times one plus redshift squared, leaving a two-power deficit relative to bolometric surface-brightness dimming37. What physical attenuation or beam-spreading mechanism accounts for the missing dimming factor without blurring images?

## ArcSecs interpretation-versus-observation checkpoint (editorial addition, R36)

- **Observed or calibrated quantities:** Redshift spectra, supernova light-curve intervals, CMB spectral shape and angular image sharpness are measured in separate experiments.
- **What the observation does not determine by itself:** It does not identify a unique fundamental physical ontology or interaction mechanism; experimental limits must remain explicitly labeled as limits.
- **ArcSecs mechanism-building task:** Investigate physically coupled photon frequency, speed and arrival interval changes under constant progression, distinguishing failed energy-only toy laws from all possible future mechanisms.
- **Reproducible comparison / sources:** DES 1504-supernova duration study, COBE/FIRAS, high-z galaxy imaging, DESI BAO.
- **Falsification rule:** Retain every published measured quantity and uncertainty. If a specified ArcSecs mechanism fails that benchmark, record the failing assumption and design a more complete physical mechanism or a discriminating observation; do not erase or recast the measurement as a successful fit.
- **Scientific release status:** Research working edition. Source bibliography, numerical calculations, author attribution and any claimed original simulations require independent scientific verification before journal submission.
