BOOK DESCRIPTION (copy into KDP)
What if the universe could be investigated from physical processes first?

BEYOND SPACETIME presents ArcSecs, a research program exploring twelve foundational propositions about light, time, physical motion, gravity and the universe. Rather than treating its premises as established discoveries, the investigation develops candidate physical mechanisms and asks what measurable consequences would distinguish them from conventional explanations.

The book follows a broad narrative of photon propagation, physical clocks, cosmic distances and relational inertia into progressively more technical mathematical studies. It examines conservation laws, finite-capacity reservoirs, positive-mass radiation models, optical transport, cosmic domains and the challenges of preserving observed spectra and timing.

This integrated edition includes the complete Scientific Review 6.0, extending through Part XXXIV on forward-coherent physical conversion and the escape criterion. It retains conditional successes, limitations, independently reproduced numerical benchmarks and open research questions.

Written for readers interested in foundational physics and alternative cosmological research, BEYOND SPACETIME is an investigation, not a claim that a new cosmology has been experimentally established. It distinguishes research premises, mathematical consequences, numerical verification and independent observations.
