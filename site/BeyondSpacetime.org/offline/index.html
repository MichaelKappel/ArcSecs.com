Scientific integrity and offline scope

The twelve charter statements are proposed research premises, not established empirical findings. Local calculations are educational mathematical controls. The offline JavaScript solver is a port of the foundation PHP solver; numerical agreement is tested within floating-point tolerance, not a claim of bitwise equality or experimental confirmation. Original English research remains authoritative.

Language content is AI-assisted educational translation from the Global Learning edition. Independent scientific translation review is still needed. No analytics, cloud sync or external runtime libraries. Your progress and recipes are local to this browser. Removing the offline cache does not erase your notebook.
