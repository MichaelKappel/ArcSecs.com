## Claim classes
**O — observation:** directly measured quantities with instrument, sample, uncertainty and published provenance. **D — derived math:** valid consequence under explicit equations/assumptions and unit checks. **I — interpretation:** an ontology or model-dependent meaning attributed to observations. **H — hypothesis:** candidate interaction not independently validated. **U — unverified:** AI-authored analysis or numerical fit without retrievable data/code/execution record. **C — conflict:** internally inconsistent source or incompatible models needing adjudication.

The nine Part II reports are detailed technical source material. Their claims that fits were executed or all sources verified are not automatically accepted at face value: original data, scripts, versions, nuisance assumptions and diagnostic outputs are not bundled. Report claims are **U pending replay** unless primary source and run artifacts are independently located.

## Required verification record per researched claim
Record `claim ID`, exact report/chapter section, scientific question, source title/DOI/publisher URL, publication date/version, author claim vs measured datum, units, method and dataset ID, negative controls, uncertainties, data/code locations, execution evidence or `NOT EXECUTED`, status (O/D/I/H/U/C), reviewer, review date, and any superseded statement. Retain disagreement with ArcSecs predictions as benchmark measurements; do not present it as proof of a literal fabric.

## Known unresolved examples
- **Photon mass:** energy and gravitational coupling are observations; nonzero Lorentz-invariant rest mass remains a distinct unconfirmed experimental question. PDG upper bounds are not detections.
- **Clock rates:** compare directly observed frequency ratios and particle decay, separate from whether a physical time substance exists. Rate scaling that matches relativistic predictions exactly is an alternative *interpretation*, not a new empirical discovery.
- **Tired light:** photon energy loss, group speed decrease and arrival-event stretching are different observables. The DES 1,504-supernova stretch measurement is a mandatory benchmark.
- **Cosmic chronology:** 13.8-Gyr standard cosmological estimate is model-dependent; this does not identify the total parent-cosmos age. Do not extrapolate brown-dwarf impostor rates to all galaxies.
- **Bubble universes:** external-pressure models, cosmic boundary geometry and other universes lack confirmed direct detection; identify causal and stress-energy assumptions.
- **Dark Matter Drive:** verify conversion units: local benchmark 0.3 GeV/cm³ is about 5.35e-22 kg/m³, NOT 0.01 kg/m³.
- **Gupta CCC+TL:** published alternative cosmology includes expansion-related equations; it does not establish a strictly static, nonmetric ArcSecs replacement. Independently verify 2026 BBN paper and exact assumptions before citing it as a decisive finding.

## Citation hygiene and publication integrity
Cite the primary paper or official data release; bibliographies in copied reports contain at least one literal `unknown_url` placeholder and may contain non-primary mirrors. These are explicitly **unverified** until replaced by exact references. Never invent DOIs, data, peer review, execution logs, institutional endorsement or precision values. Report AI use, provenance and limitations. Existing citations in the book are a starting registry, not a guarantee of current URL resolution.
