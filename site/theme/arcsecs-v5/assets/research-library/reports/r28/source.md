### **Challenge 1: How can ArcSecs claim "light slows down over time" (Premise 3\) when GW170817 shows gravitational waves and gamma rays arriving within 1.74 seconds after an 85-to-130-million-year flight?**

**Response:** This is a formidable empirical constraint (Table 7, REQ-01)5. If photons slow monotonically with flight duration while quadrupole gravitational waves maintain constant speed, a fractional slowing even as tiny as one part in ten to the fifteenth over 100 million years would already violate the GW170817 arrival coincidence. Consequently, Premise 3 can survive REQ-01 only under one of three strictly testable conditions: both photons and gravitational waves experience the exact same secular propagation slowing over cosmic progression (preserving a common arrival time while both decelerate relative to an uncoupled universal standard); photon slowing is nonlinearly suppressed in the low-redshift local universe (under 40 megaparsecs, redshift \~0.0099 for NGC 499379) and manifests only across deeper gigaparsec or high-density sightlines; or the slowing rate is smaller than parts in ten to the sixteenth per hundred million years—which would make it far too small to account for cosmological redshifts on its own. We explicitly mark the microphysical mechanism of Premise 3 as an **open, currently missing mechanism** awaiting derivation and high-redshift multimessenger tests.

### **Challenge 2: Your Machian void-inertia calculation shows that an unscreened inverse-distance kernel changes inertia by less than 0.005 percent inside a 30-megaparsec void, while short-range Yukawa screening clashes with Smiciklas et al. (2011) by 27 orders of magnitude. Does this eliminate Machian void-drive proposals?**

**Response:** It strongly rules out **anisotropic tensor** Machian inertia couplings to nuclear spin and **unscreened** void-drive shortcuts, and we report that verdict candidly rather than concealing it3. Even for a strictly **scalar** (isotropic) Yukawa-screened inertia law—which bypasses the directional nuclear-spin comagnetometer bound because scalar mass shifts affect all orientations identically—a 50-megaparsec screening length predicts a 3.8 percent difference in baryonic inertial mass between the Milky Way disk and intergalactic space, and a 6 to 10 percent reduction inside deep voids5. That scalar hypothesis remains testable and makes a sharp, immediately falsifiable prediction for the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation of void galaxies versus wall galaxies (Experiment EXP-E4 in EXPERIMENTS.csv). If void galaxies obey the identical baryonic mass-velocity scaling to within 1 percent, short-range scalar Machian void-inertia reduction is falsified as well.

### **Challenge 3: You invoke Mansouri-Sexl and Selleri constant-simultaneity test theories to eliminate causal loops for FTL signaling. Don't those theories require physical length contraction and clock slowing relative to a preferred frame—and if they reproduce all round-trip tests, aren't they empirically indistinguishable from special relativity?**

**Response:** Exact Selleri transformations with a single universal light speed are empirically equivalent to special relativity for all closed-loop two-way electromagnetic tests, differing only in one-way synchronization convention—unless **either** a non-electromagnetic physical signal propagates faster than light (Architecture 2), which physically defines absolute simultaneity and immediately breaks operational equivalence with relativity, **or** physical rod contraction and atomic-clock rate compensation are slightly imperfect at high precision, leaving a residual sidereal modulation in one-way portable-clock-calibrated links (Experiment EXP-E1) or optical-clock campaigns1. Our experimental program targets both exact breaking points rather than relying on unobservable synchronization conventions.

### **Challenge 4: Under constant universal "when," you state that muon lifetimes (64.419 microseconds at CERN) and millimeter-scale strontium clock shifts reflect physical rate changes in matter rather than time dilation. Why do completely different physical forces—weak nuclear decay in muons, optical electronic transitions in strontium, and hyperfine transitions in hydrogen masers—all shift by the exact same quantitative factor?**

**Response:** This universality of physical rate shifts across electromagnetic and weak-interaction clocks (historically termed local position invariance and universality of clock rates) is the single deepest microphysical challenge facing ArcSecs Premises 2 and 4 (Table 7, REQ-04)19. In a non-spacetime physical-first theory, universal rate scaling across different interactions can arise only if all mass-bearing fields share a common physical coupling to the local gravitational potential and to motion relative to the cosmic matter frame (for example, through a universal physical scaling of effective inertial mass or local field propagation constants entering every Hamiltonian). No complete ArcSecs derivation of that universal coupling currently exists; we explicitly classify it as part of the missing physical mechanism (Section 5.4) and highlight precision multi-species clock comparisons (comparing optical, hyperfine, nuclear, and weak-decay clocks across altitude and velocity) as the direct test of whether universality holds exactly or breaks at parts in ten to the twentieth.

### **Challenge 5: If photons have mass (Premise 1), why doesn't Coulomb's law fail in laboratories or solar-wind magnetic fields collapse, and why do astrophysical bounds constrain invariant rest mass near 10⁻⁵¹ kilograms?**

**Response:** First, ArcSecs Premise 1 begins from the uncontested experimental fact that photons carry energy, transfer linear momentum, and respond to gravity. Second, regarding the narrower claim of **nonzero invariant rest mass**, Williams, Faller, and Hill (1971)37, Goldhaber and Nieto (2010)36, and extragalactic radio pulsar/FRB analyses7 establish only upper bounds—never strict zero. As our executed calculation shows5, an invariant photon rest mass of 1.0 × 10⁻⁵² kilograms satisfies every laboratory Coulomb and FRB dispersion bound comfortably while remaining strictly nonzero. What those bounds *do* constrain is that any invariant Proca rest mass cannot produce macroscopic slowing of optical light across the universe (\~2.5 parts in ten to the thirty-first fractional shift at optical frequencies5), forcing Premise 3 to seek a non-Proca physical interaction mechanism.

### **Challenge 6: Couldn't the \~5-sigma CatWISE2020 quasar dipole anomaly simply be an unmodeled infrared selection bias or local large-scale clustering rather than a genuine preferred-frame mismatch with the CMB?**

**Response:** Yes—systematic ecliptic scan-depth artifacts and large-scale structure clustering remain active, legitimate counter-hypotheses in the astrophysical literature34. Instead, we note that independent NVSS radio galaxies—which suffer entirely different instrumental systematics from mid-infrared WISE detectors and share zero overlapping objects—independently reject the kinematic CMB null hypothesis13. Using both the Planck CMB axis and the primary-table CatWISE2020 axis as pre-registered directional priors in our blind MOJAVE holdout split (EXP-H1) and continental optical-clock campaign (EXP-E1) turns an astrophysical anomaly into a clean, falsifiable kinematic test.

**Response:** Affirmative scientific research must never omit material empirical constraints. Alväger et al. **Response:** Correct—a 224-jet holdout split with 0.45-dex intrinsic viewing-angle scatter cannot detect a 0.1 percent preferred-frame shift5. ## ArcSecs interpretation-versus-observation checkpoint (editorial addition, R28)

- **Observed or calibrated quantities:** Coordinate recession estimates and projected source speeds are not direct demonstrations of local superluminal signal transport.
- **What the observation does not determine by itself:** It does not identify a unique fundamental physical ontology or interaction mechanism; experimental limits must remain explicitly labeled as limits.
- **ArcSecs mechanism-building task:** Specify two physical endpoints, a clock protocol, and a falsifiable causal communication test under the proposed constant universal progression.
- **Reproducible comparison / sources:** VLBI jet kinematics, FRB localization, optical timing, multimessenger source delays.
- **Falsification rule:** Retain every published measured quantity and uncertainty. If a specified ArcSecs mechanism fails that benchmark, record the failing assumption and design a more complete physical mechanism or a discriminating observation; do not erase or recast the measurement as a successful fit.
- **Scientific release status:** Research working edition. Source bibliography, numerical calculations, author attribution and any claimed original simulations require independent scientific verification before journal submission.
