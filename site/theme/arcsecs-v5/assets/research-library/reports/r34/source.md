Extending thin-shell matching to arbitrary gravitational actions depending on curvature invariants and their covariant derivatives, Senovilla (2026) demonstrated via distributional calculus that thin shells arise from jumps in the (m+1)-th normal derivative of the Riemann tensor, that purely quadratic theories uniquely permit gravitational double layers, and that shell-free proper matchings universally require continuity of normal bulk energy-momentum components across every diffeomorphism-invariant theory9.

### **Model A: Classical Material Bubble Baseline**

Consider a spherical physical interface of instantaneous radius separating an interior medium at uniform pressure from an exterior environment at another pressure, carrying positive surface-energy density (tension) in joules per square meter and surface inertia per unit area. Under quasistatic assumptions, total potential energy equals the surface-area tension energy cost minus the volume pressure-work gain given by the interior-minus-exterior pressure difference multiplied by enclosed sphere volume. Differentiating energy with respect to radius shows two competing radial forces: an inward restoring surface-tension force proportional to radius and surface-energy density, and a pressure-difference force proportional to surface area and the pressure excess inside over outside. Equilibrium defines the Laplace balance, where pressure difference equals twice surface tension divided by radius, establishing an unstable critical nucleation radius.  
Below the critical radius, surface tension dominates and the bubble collapses; above it, positive interior-minus-exterior pressure difference drives runaway radial acceleration. Including wall inertia in the Euler–Lagrange evolution equation ensures conservation of total kinetic plus surface plus volume potential energy3. Notice that if the exterior medium possesses *more negative* pressure than the interior, then interior pressure minus exterior pressure is strictly positive, producing an outward force across the interface. Calling this outward acceleration "exterior gravitational attraction" is physically mistaken: it is a local stress-gradient force across a contact wall, identical in mechanism to an overpressured vapor bubble expanding in a lower-pressure fluid. Furthermore, the three-dimensional thermal nucleation barrier scales with the cube of surface tension divided by the square of pressure difference, whereas four-dimensional semiclassical quantum tunneling (Coleman 1977\) scales with the fourth power of surface tension divided by the cube of vacuum energy difference and cannot be interchanged without altering action dimensionality2.

### **Model B: First-Order Vacuum Phase Transition (Historical Benchmark)**

In standard scalar-field vacuum decay (Coleman 1977; Coleman & De Luccia 1980), two homogeneous field minima have unequal potential energy densities, forming a metastable false vacuum outside and a lower-energy true vacuum inside2. Semiclassical tunneling nucleates a critical true-vacuum bubble whose growth converts higher-energy false vacuum into lower-energy true vacuum, transferring the liberated bulk energy directly into the kinetic energy of the accelerating wall2. Gravitational backreaction introduces bulk curvature terms that enlarge the bounce action and, if true-vacuum energy is negative, risk a post-tunneling collapse rather than eternal growth23. Instead, the higher energy density and more negative pressure of the exterior false vacuum relative to the interior true vacuum create a positive outward energy-conversion gradient; gravitational effects of the false vacuum inhibit or curve the background rather than pulling the wall outward like a surrounding attracting shell2.

### **F. Five Rigorous Referee Challenges and Data-Grounded Responses (REFEREE\_CHALLENGES.md Equivalent)**

> 1. **Objection 1 (Shell Theorem Refutes Isotropic Exterior Pull):** *Referee:* "If Newton's shell theorem cancels all isotropic exterior gravitational forces inside a cavity, does that not immediately disprove the entire Mission 07 hypothesis of outside gravity pulling a cosmic bubble larger?" *Response:* It rules out *isotropic inverse-square gravitational pull* as the growth engine, which we report transparently as a falsified claim3. However, it does not rule out either an exterior-to-interior physical stress or scalar-matter gradient across a real material wall or an asymmetric exterior mass distribution inducing bulk dipole acceleration plus quadrupole tidal shear—both of which yield quantitative, falsifiable observational bounds3.  
> 2. **Objection 2 (Distance Floor versus Shear Ceiling):** *Referee:* "Your own scaling requires any asymmetric exterior mass explaining a 10⁻³ dipole to sit beyond ten million Hubble radii to satisfy Planck's 10⁻¹⁰ shear limit. Does that not push the exterior source beyond causal testability?" *Response:* Yes—for an unscreened inverse-square asymmetric exterior attractor, the ten-million Hubble-radius floor severely restricts observability and rules out nearby unscreened tidal pull models3. Only if an exterior dipole arises from a genuine local boundary stress gradient, or if an interior observed dipole such as CatWISE6 couples to a measurable sub-horizon shear mode, does the hypothesis remain empirically testable.  
> 3. **Objection 3 (Photon Rest Mass Tension):** *Referee:* "How can ArcSecs maintain 'photons have mass' and 'light slows down over time' when PDG and FRB observations constrain photon rest mass below 10⁻⁵¹ to 10⁻⁵⁴ kg?" *Response:* We explicitly distinguish the ArcSecs premise that photons carry physical mass-energy, momentum, and gravitational responsiveness from an unverified nonzero Proca invariant rest mass5. Any Proca mass above 3.9 × 10⁻⁵¹ kg is ruled out by FRB arrival times5; therefore, any viable ArcSecs light-slowing mechanism must be formulated as an achromatic momentum-exchange or energy-loss interaction whose dispersive lag stays below FRB detection limits.  
> 4. **Objection 4 (Backdoor Reinstallation of Pressure as Gravity):** *Referee:* "Isn't Model D's relational gradient simply renaming the classical pressure difference?" *Response:* Precisely for that reason, Section 5 and the Compliance Statement refuse to label a radial pressure or field gradient "exterior gravity." We classify Model D's radial gradient as a non-gravitational contact or stress-energy interaction across a physical interface and isolate gravitational effects strictly to asymmetric tidal shear or unverified finite-range screening3.  
> 5. **Objection 5 (Absence of Any Detected Bubble Wall or Collision):** *Referee:* "With WMAP and Planck constraining observable bubble collisions to fewer than four at 95 percent confidence and no cosmic wall detected, what empirical justification remains for studying bubble boundaries?" *Response:* Current null limits constrain only *observable thin-wall collisions intersecting our past electromagnetic visibility cone* under specific scalar-potential priors4. Testing whether directional CMB anomalies47 and quasar dipole excesses6 exhibit aligned shear correlations remains a valid, falsifiable observational program whose null outcome further bounds exterior inhomogeneities without relying on speculative metric fabrics3.

## ArcSecs interpretation-versus-observation checkpoint (editorial addition, R34)

- **Observed or calibrated quantities:** Physical pressure differences can accelerate a material boundary; symmetric surrounding inverse-square gravity cancels inside a spherical shell.
- **What the observation does not determine by itself:** It does not identify a unique fundamental physical ontology or interaction mechanism; experimental limits must remain explicitly labeled as limits.
- **ArcSecs mechanism-building task:** Test nonmetric exterior screened interactions, boundary pressure gradients and anisotropic tides separately; do not relabel exterior gravity as a measured isotropic pull.
- **Reproducible comparison / sources:** Classical shell theorem, vacuum-wall literature, CMB anisotropy bounds.
- **Falsification rule:** Retain every published measured quantity and uncertainty. If a specified ArcSecs mechanism fails that benchmark, record the failing assumption and design a more complete physical mechanism or a discriminating observation; do not erase or recast the measurement as a successful fit.
