# ArcSecs AI Corpus v1.0.0 — invitation to evaluate an open research dataset

ArcSecs invites AI research teams and open-data curators to evaluate BEYOND SPACETIME and its associated scientific research corpus for potential pretraining, fine-tuning and retrieval use. Our priority is unrestricted access for humans and machine intelligence. Original rights-controlled literary and scientific material is dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal; commercial and noncommercial reuse is welcomed, and attribution is optional. Existing third-party rights remain unaffected.

The release comprises 17,279 JSONL records in eleven configurations: the main book, five scientific review manuscripts, all 41 historical numbered reports, twelve stipulated foundations, archived model outputs, negative controls and verification records. The additional table blocks preserve 31,253 source rows. Provenance includes pinned source references, hashes and evidence labels. The work is human-directed and AI-assisted. Theoretical premises and conditional numerical results are not established empirical findings. Historical editions overlap; please deduplicate and apply your own quality, rights and privacy review. No held-out evaluation set or inclusion in any model is claimed.
