## Governing model

In normalized units with wave speed 1, evolve `phi_T = pi`, `pi_T = phi_xx + phi - phi^3`, with potential `V(phi)=(phi^2-1)^2/4`. The two vacua are phi = ±1. A periodic nearest-neighbor spatial lattice of length 64 has 256, 512 or 1024 cells. Kick–drift–kick advances the discrete Hamiltonian with dt <= dx/4. For state 1 the initial interface pair is the difference of two tanh profiles minus one, separated by d; their inward velocities are ±v and the approximate boosted width is sqrt(2(1-v^2)). This superposition is not an exact two-wall solution. State 0 is the constant vacuum -1; state 2 initializes a compact polynomial pulse of half-width 1.5, centered at -d/2, initially at rest. The velocity setting affects only state 1, amplitude only state 2. Normalized parameters are illustrative. The periodic boundary can return disturbances. A finite stencil has a numerical domain of dependence, not an exact continuum light cone. The kink pair has noncompact tails. No particle coupling, gravitational backreaction, 3D geometry, photon transport, or continuum physical collision prediction is included. No exact continuum momentum-conservation claim is made.

## Independent local energy accounting

Cell energy density is `e_i = pi_i^2/2 + V(phi_i) + ((phi_i-phi_(i-1))^2+(phi_(i+1)-phi_i)^2)/(4 dx^2)`. Half of each bond energy belongs to each adjacent cell. The corresponding right-face flux is `J_(i+1/2) = -(pi_i+pi_(i+1))(phi_(i+1)-phi_i)/(2 dx)`. The semidiscrete equations give `de_i/dT = (J_left-J_right)/dx`. The center region is the half-open set of grid cells with centers x in [-2,2); central energy is the sum of cell energies times dx. Integrate inward flux (left face minus right face) by the trapezoidal rule at every integration step, independently of the measured central energy change. Report both `E_total(T)-E_total(0)` and `E_center(T)-E_center(0)-integral J_in dT`. Residuals are finite integration errors. Profile snapshots capture the initial, highest central-energy integration step, and final fields. JSON evidence exports these snapshots, parameters, and all sampled energy/flux values. Maximum residuals are taken across integration steps, not only plotted samples.

## Numerical verification

The independent fixture generator uses SciPy DOP853 to solve the same semidiscrete spatial problem and evolves the flux integral as an additional ODE. Five trajectories (collision, pulse, stationary, vacuum, faster collision), 1065 time samples, nine quantities per sample (including time), and 2560 final spatial field values were compared. Absolute tolerance is 4e-4 for sampled non-time values and 1e-4 for final field values, with time tolerance 1e-10; observed maxima were below those limits. The independent solver is not a continuum or observational reference. Additional tests cover vacuum, exact early grid isolation, zero-amplitude null, stationary pair, mirror symmetry, temporal and spatial refinement, stress parameters, determinism, partial final step and invalid inputs. Default global relative residual is about 2.305e-4; maximum absolute local residual about 4.809e-4. Halving dt reduces the latter to about 1.203e-4. At model time 10.9 the plotted central energy is approximately 2.152 of 2.177 initial total energy; by time 22 it is about 0.0170. These are normalized modeled energy values, not physical cosmic energy measurements.
