# v3.142.0 — Integrated Scientific Simulation release candidate

## New controls and provenance

- **Chapter 32A OF1–OF8** (`outflow`): closed-form prescribed exponential material flow with constant characteristic, fixed emission energy and gap. The selected energy redshift determines the separation, not a fit. The arrival derivative holds separation fixed; medium-relative and common-coordinate velocities are separate. The zero-gap unit-survival case recovers distance duality. Positive-gap cases need not; the common-coordinate speed can exceed the characteristic under Galilean advection, which is a limitation, not demonstrated superluminal signaling. Original: `tools/entrained_outflow_v1.py` and `docs/long-term-memory/research/outflow/scan.csv`.
- **T-01/T-03** (`clockCalibration`): integrate positive prescribed linear clock rates over emitter and receiver intervals. Clock count stretch differs from coordinate stretch and the calibrated spectroscopic energy ratio. Independent SR inertial and first-order weak-field clock comparisons are controls, not a new constitutive law. The supplied local receiver-line ratio calibrates the supplied medium-frame frequency ratio; no additional Doppler model. A global simulation tick is bookkeeping, not proof of absolute time.
- **Chapter 30N** (`quantumReceiver`): direct port of `tools/quantum_receiver_v1.py` with mobile centers, two Bloch vectors, homogeneous scalar and 16–192 transverse periodic modes. RK4 differs from Python's DOP853; the screened line surrogate is bounded to 0.045–0.060. Two-level coherent emission, no spontaneous emission, no full longitudinal or 3D quantum field theory. Gaussian tails and Fourier truncation prevent a sharp causal-front claim. Energy, field momentum and Bloch norms are checked; the seven fresh reference trajectories are sampled every 0.2 model-time units. Archived 28 spectral rows and four controls are checked, not merely displayed.
- **Chapter 14 / Appendix E8** (`driveBudget`): new nonrelativistic steady ship-frame engineering model extending the book's intake estimate. All captured mass is exhausted; incoming kinetic energy dissipates as heat. Power available after confinement limits exhaust kinetic power by efficiency. Thrust = mass flow × (exhaust speed − intake speed) − extra drag. Confinement and conversion losses are heat. Ideal one-sided effective radiating area = heat / [emissivity × Stefan–Boltzmann constant × (T⁴ − 3⁴)]. Additional opposing drag is prescribed and not included in a global-frame energy ledger. No actual substrate capture, field power, shielding, radiator structure or measured thrust is derived. Both speed caps are 0.01c; relativistic propulsion is outside this control. Zero capture cannot generate reaction thrust. Energy and momentum residuals check the defined bookkeeping identities, not independent physical validity.

## Experiment reproducibility

The cosmic laboratory has 17 experiments. All accept bounded controls, export full JSON evidence and numeric CSV rows, and export compact recipes. Recipes may be loaded from current/older `arcsecs.cosmic-experiment.v1` evidence or `arcsecs.cosmic-recipe.v1`; only the model and exact numerical parameter keys are used. Supplied result data are ignored, and users explicitly run a new calculation. The original version is not emulated automatically. File size is bounded to 8 MiB. Invalid schema, model, key sets, nonfinite or out-of-range values are rejected. Model-specific cross-parameter validation occurs at Run. CSV includes all returned rows, not every underlying integration substep; profile arrays and full metadata remain in JSON. The previous 29 worker scenario numerical baselines are retained.
